Frequently asked questions

A PERM labor certification requires employers to place two Sunday newspaper ads in a newspaper of general circulation in the area of intended employment. These ads must run on two different Sundays and include the job title, duties, requirements, and employer contact information as specified by DOL regulations (20 CFR § 656.17).
Costs vary by market and newspaper. Typical range is $300–$3,000 per ad depending on the newspaper's circulation size, the ad's word count, and current rate card. We publish real pricing for each market — no hidden markups or surprise fees.
It depends on state law. A growing number of states (including California, New York, Colorado, and Washington) require salary ranges in job postings, which includes PERM recruitment ads. Check our Wage Transparency Laws page for the current list.
From the start of recruitment to the earliest filing date is typically 60–75 days, depending on when your Sunday ad dates fall. This includes the SWA job order (30 days), two Sunday newspaper ads, and the mandatory 30-day quiet period. Use our Timeline Calculator to get exact dates.
DOL requires the ad to run in a "newspaper of general circulation" in the area of intended employment. This is typically the major daily/Sunday newspaper for that metro area. Our city pages list the appropriate newspaper for each market we cover.
